# Provenance and licensing

- Measured projections: Alexander Meaney, **Cone-Beam Computed Tomography Dataset of a Walnut**, University of Helsinki, [Zenodo record 6986012](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6986012), CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The distributed fixtures and previews are modified: uniform angular subsampling, optional five-pixel circular left shift, intensity binning, per-view air normalization, negative logarithm, and FDK reconstruction. The original data are already dark/flat corrected. The duplicate final view is omitted. No affiliation or endorsement is implied. See `fixtures/walnut-s8-b8/frames.json` for individual TIFF SHA-256.
- Geometry, metadata and rotation-center note: https://zenodo.org/records/6986012 . Background ROI convention: https://github.com/Diagonalizable/HelTomo .
- L. A. Feldkamp, L. C. Davis, J. W. Kress, “Practical cone-beam algorithm,” JOSA A 1, 612–619 (1984), doi:10.1364/JOSAA.1.000612.
- External reference only: [ASTRA Toolbox FDK_CUDA](https://astra-toolbox.com/docs/algs/FDK_CUDA.html), GPL-3.0. The native executable does not link to ASTRA and uses no ASTRA kernel. Comparative Python scripts use a separately installed ASTRA distribution.
- NVIDIA driver, NVRTC and cuFFT are external NVIDIA software subject to NVIDIA terms. No NVIDIA DLL is redistributed here. The core uses the CUDA Driver API (https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cuda-driver-api/) and cuFFT (https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/cufft/).

The two `CFDKMG01` files in `examples/gb10-walnut/` are modified, reconstructed subvolumes of Meaney's measured walnut dataset cited above. One contains direct native FDK output; the other contains independent ASTRA reconstructions cropped at the same voxel centers. Both derived data files are distributed under CC BY 4.0. The original acquisition and its authors do not certify these reconstructions.

The two float32 peak-plane slices in `examples/physical-peak-walnut/planes.cfdk` and the derived peak-difference profile are modified reconstructions/statistics of the same Alexander Meaney walnut acquisition (University of Helsinki, Zenodo 6986012, CC BY 4.0). They compare direct native FDK with same-grid ASTRA FDK. These derived data and their profile are distributed under CC BY 4.0. Neither the acquisition authors nor ASTRA endorse this comparison.

The completed evidence pack `examples/physical-peak-batch/planes.cfdk` includes derived raw peak-plane pairs from the walnut acquisition credited above and from Alexander Meaney, **Cone-Beam Computed Tomography Dataset of a Pine Cone**, University of Helsinki, [Zenodo record 6985407](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6985407), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Pine projections were binned 8×8, air-normalized, log-transformed and reconstructed with native FDK and ASTRA, at 90 and 720 angles. The two SVGs plot per-plane intensity differences. These derived slices and profiles are distributed under CC BY 4.0. No endorsement or physical dimensional calibration is implied.
