Does the measured accuracy of the frozen raw-profile method persist elsewhere within the same three scans? Selected by availability and separation from calibration [160,184), familiar transfer [256,352), and formal [400,528); do not inspect new labels before the public seal. Apply the same checked numeric CPU and CUDA paths on all new voxels, reject nonfinite scores or any CPU/GPU classification discrepancy. Record raw/archive/field/weight/mask SHA256, physical spacing, shape, origin and parity-check counts. Seal all six complete masks remotely before reading any new target segmentation slices.
