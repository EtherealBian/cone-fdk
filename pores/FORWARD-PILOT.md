# Does a shared 3-D image-formation operator improve the digital boundary?

Development 2/3/5 only. The signed-volume screen conflated a boundary estimate with a scalar pore size, and often abstained. Instead test a voxel-level forward model: predict gray mixtures from neighboring occupancy, then test localized evidence for a foreground/background flip. This is an *effective* kernel relative to archived digital labels, not an estimate of the scanner's physical PSF. No reserve data, formal H rescore, per-ID selection, tuning on transfer or transfer-label input. Never manufacture a confirmation score from any step below.

**Calibration forward check.** On each original [160,184) calibration slab, x-local [0,120) for fit, [136,256) for check, y-local [0,256) throughout. The intervening 16 columns are unused. Evaluate sites touching the other phase in six-connectivity; exclude a two-voxel margin on all six faces of each partition. In the fit partition use separate uint16 medians of pore/material at Euclidean distance >3 from opposite phase and exterior (at least 64 samples each), to express native raw as unbounded pore fraction (material - observed)/(material - pore). Same numbers for check; no check-side normalization. Fit one shared, isotropic, nonnegative unit-sum kernel over squared lattice distance shells 0,1,2,3,4 (1/6/12/8/6 sites). Minimize mean squared error from a weighted sum of shell occupancy means to the normalized raw target, equally weighting each scan and each side of the boundary. Use deterministic least squares on the simplex (enumerate all 31 nonempty active sets, retain nonnegative optimizers; use lowest objective, tie by smallest bitmask). Quantize shell masses to sum 4096 by floor plus largest remainder, tie by smaller distance. Always use quantized mass for prediction. Control is mass 4096 at center. Publish fit code/phase medians/weights before decoding right-side labels for the check. Score native CUDA shell occupancy against independent CPU implementation; require identical integer outputs. Require >=128 sites per phase per scan, >=10% lower balanced RMSE on **each** scan vs center-only control, no increase in either phase's RMSE, no zero-contrast or sample-count failures. Report all six side errors and gate failures. On failure stop *this mechanism*, not the project.

**Only if the forward check passes**, apply both operators, unchanged, to all three existing development transfer census masks. Estimate phase endpoints from the raw transfer crop and deep regions of its **predicted** census mask with exactly the same depth/count/positive contrast requirement (fall back to unchanged census if insufficient). No per-scan labels or per-scan tuning in deployment. Compute one simultaneous six-neighbor regularized update, never feed updates back into the same step. With m the census mask, u the unbounded normalized raw, K the operator, S=sum(K^2), and n the number of positive face neighbors, predict 1 when

`2*Kᵀ(u-K*m) + (2*m-1)*S + S*(0.5*(2*n/6-1) + 0.5*(2*m-1)) > 0`.

Exact ties retain the census decision. Leave the four-voxel crop margin untouched because two convolutions of radius two need real support. No integration by original size, component guard, size clamp, or exclusion of original background. Freeze all six masks, hashes of inputs, weights, endpoint counts and decision agreement with independently implemented CPU arithmetic *before* scoring transfer labels. On every scan report census/center/blur 11 gates, both object coverages, all-matched and common-reference P95 and gained/lost ID lists, native 8/37 and independent SciPy checks. Advance only if blur vs **both** census and center: paired same-ID volume P95 improves by >=2 percentage points (>=5 common), both coverage fractions do not fall, Dice does not fall by >.002, and CPU/native decisions agree everywhere. Check strengths and failure cases without changing FINAL-GATE. Positive forward prediction alone is not segmentation accuracy.
