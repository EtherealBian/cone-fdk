# Does the original calibration contain distinct 3-D volume-residual targets?

Calibration-only feasibility check, not a new segmentation model, endpoint choice or transfer score. Use only z=[160,184) of 2/3/5, the published held-out detector mask for each, and that slab's original segmented archive. No retuning of the folds, no raw migration data, migration labels, other depths, or 4/6. Never infer the volume missing behind a crop face.

Label in 6-connectivity. On *both* sides require >=64 observed voxels and no contact with any lateral (x/y) wall. Keep both fully interior objects and those clipped on z, but a pair must have exactly the same contact flags at z-low and z-high. Match one-to-one by decreasing exact intersection/union >=1/2, then increasing predicted and reference raster labels. Publish eligible totals, unmatched IDs, all pairs with class, observed volumes, intersection/union and signed volume residual (reference minus detector); do not use eval IDs. Report complete and z-cut targets separately. Equal counts count as a tie; an absolute relative residual exactly 15% is within tolerance. Count pairs, not slices or independently sampled voxels. Cropped targets supervise *observed* mass only, and pairs in one scan are not independent experiments.

Prespecified feasibility criterion for a future observed-volume residual model: each calibration specimen supplies at least five distinct paired structures, including at least two underestimates and two overestimates, complete+z-cut combined. This is only a minimum directional-support test, not a power calculation, evidence that any selector generalizes, or a relaxation of the unclipped evaluation rule. Fail => do not justify a new selector using this supervision alone as if every scan had sufficient support. Do not relax matching/clipping/size after the count. Restart from source archives and sealed masks twice; compare the complete per-structure records byte-for-byte. Validate native CUDA component counts, volumes and clipping on both masks, not only aggregate counts. No reserved data regardless of result.
