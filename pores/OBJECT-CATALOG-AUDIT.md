# Can selecting intact objects from the published methods even explain the two dominant failures?

Diagnostic only, not a candidate. On the already seen transfer crop z=[256,352), use **all** named arms in these twelve studies: section-area, axial-raw, census-train, local-connectivity, two-scale, five-plane, small-seed, mass, forward, monotone, orientation and distance-target. Include failed methods. Identify repeated masks by full byte digest per specimen and verify that their object ledgers agree; they must not become additional votes. Require identical reference-mask digest, all eligible IDs and reference volumes for every comparator. No formal archives, reserve specimens, fitting, recomputing masks, confidence thresholds or synthetic joins of fragments.

For each eligible reference ID publish the number of unique masks in which it is matched, all matched predictions' sizes and per-mask error, the least absolute relative volume error over those matches, and the reference IDs never matched. Use the original one-to-one, >=64, unclipped and IoU >=0.5 assignments, with both sides kept unchanged. As a deliberately optimistic test allow a selector to know the true reference ID and volume and to pick any previously matched **intact** prediction per reference; ignore conflicting predictions, unmatched predictions and within-mask consistency. The union of IDs is thus its maximum possible reference coverage. To reach >=90%, let k=ceil(9*N/10) by integer arithmetic. If union < k the joint objective is impossible in this restricted catalog. Else compute the lowest possible linear-interpolation P95 of absolute volume error among selections covering >=k: take the k smallest of the per-ID minima. Also report the P95 over the entire union and coverage/errors for every unmixed input mask. The minimum over subsets follows by order statistics: the j-th smallest in a subset is at least the j-th smallest overall, and along a sorted prefix linear P95 does not decrease as its length increases. Compare with 15% exactly; no rounding for decisions.

Verify independently from each arm's CSV (not just precomputed booleans); test the lower-bound calculation against exhaustive enumeration on small cases. Per specimen report separately whether the *two* conditions are even jointly possible in this relaxed best case, and whether any one unmodified mask already meets those two conditions. Other nine gates are not tested by this diagnostic. A negative result rules out only selection among these original assignments, not predicting new masks, resolving merges or global rematching. A positive result neither defines a deployable selector nor validates an ensemble. Use the missing IDs / volume outliers to separate missing candidates from ranking and boundary error; never count the catalog ceiling as a promotion.
