# Can integrated attenuation distinguish undersized pores from genuinely small pores?

Development only, 2/3/5. The previously examined gray-growth experiment found 3 matches among 36 entrants; 23 of 33 unmatched entrants primarily overlapped complete reference pores below 64. Do not select IDs. Also test whether the *same* unrounded volume estimate helps the boundaries of already measurable pores. No reserve access, formal evaluation, changes to the gate, or per-scan choice of arms. No reference-assisted deployment claim. This is an approximate gray-space mixture model on reconstructed uint16, not an absolute attenuation calibration or a physical volume standard.

1. Calibration is confined to z=[160,184) in the original xy crop on each development scan. For pore and material separately use all labeled sites at Euclidean distance >3 from the opposite phase **and** >3 from the exterior of the calibration box (exterior lattice sites begin one voxel past the face). Record counts and doubled raw medians P2 and M2; abstain for this specimen if either count <64 or M2<=P2. Touching pore fragments may supply interior samples, never whole-object target volumes. No calibration on other slabs. No parameter selection.
2. Start from sealed transfer census and previous raw-gray proposal on [256,352). Analyze **every** complete original component of 8..512 voxels. Let d be Euclidean center distance to that original support. Require its bounding box expanded by six on every side to be inside the observed ROI. Abstain if any foreign original foreground has d<=6; avoid ambiguous integration, do not fill missing pixels. Integration support I: d<=3 including the original pore; material annulus A: 4<d<=6. Let B be mean uint16 in A. Estimated voxel volume V = sum_I(B-raw)/(B-P2/2). Use signed contributions, no clipping/darkest-voxel selection. Abstain on nonpositive denominator. Record sums and counts, exact integer numerator/denominator, original and proposed sizes and abstentions. Use rational decisions including exact 64. Cross-check the integer integration by an independent per-voxel accumulation. No modification to census for abstentions in the signal arms.
3. Three paired arms: **mass**: for original 8..63, adopt the entire prior gray proposal only if it crosses 64 AND V>=64. Otherwise retain the original. **size**: same number of promotions on each scan; take the largest original seeds among all previous crossing proposals (tie by ascending original native ID); take their same full gray masks. This control uses the count, not signal for ranking, and may take an object rejected by the mass arm. **boundary**: start from mass and additionally adjust original complete 64..512 objects with valid estimates. Round V to nearest integer (ties upward); limit change to +/-floor(original/4) and a single original six-face boundary layer. Add darkest original raw outer-face voxels or remove brightest inner-face voxels (tie by x-fastest index); skip deletions that would disconnect the remaining six-connected object. Do not go back to skipped sites; never force final size >=64. The six-voxel neighbor guard keeps interventions separated. No baseline mergers, splits, or change in face status allowed. Report actual edits and remaining abstentions, including all objects outside this range as untouched.
4. Push this protocol before fitting. Publish per-scan calibration, every candidate record, selected IDs, all nine predictions, software identity, parent masks and source hashes **before loading transfer labels for this run**. Then remeasure census and all arms on source annotations (native slabs 8/37, independent SciPy; 11 original gates, both coverage denominators, all-matched P95, plus same-reference P95 and gained/lost IDs). Cross-tabulate each of the 36 former gray entrants, including retained true rescues and rejected genuine sub-64 references. Prespecified mass screen: rescue on >=2 scans, zero new unmatched on all, and strictly more total true rescues than size at identical per-scan promotion counts (also show each scan). Boundary screen: mass must pass, boundary must lose no census match or reduce either coverage fraction on any scan vs census, all three paired common-reference P95 nonincreasing vs census and strictly improved on >=2 scans. Both are mechanism screens, not FINAL-GATE. Publish every outcome, including failure/degenerate abstention. No retuning on transfer labels.
