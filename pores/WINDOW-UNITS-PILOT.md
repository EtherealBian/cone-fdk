# Historical NLM window units: separated calibration test

Thermo Fisher's [Amira–Avizo 2019.2 release notes, Compatibility notes, p. 8 of the PDF](https://documents.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/MSD/Support-Files/Amira-Avizo-2019.2_ReleaseNotes.pdf) document a parameter conversion for the *former GPU mode* around Avizo 9.7, including a change in how windows and similarity values are expressed. PDF SHA-256: `76e423bb5a4fb82a4438a2165ed761610c2e9c1a7e4b2b15c2f6d3f640372fb5`. The 2017 NIST paper describes window **sizes** 21 and 5; the archive metadata does not establish the old software mode. The reference does not say to use bigger windows on these CT images. Here we ask whether a *competing interpretation* improves held-apart calibration, not which proprietary implementation was used. No new upstream images or binaries redistributed.

Compare the usual square search/patch radii (10,2) to (21,2), (10,5), (21,5), i.e. widths (21,5), (43,5), (21,11), (43,11). All use dense square XY pixelwise NLM, self included, uniform mean patch SSD, exp(-meanSSD/h²), a 3³ median on native uint16, strict mapped byte decision, published contrast. No spatial prior, sigma subtraction, overlap voting or per-pore adjustment. The h grid in mapped units is **15,20,25,30,35,40** for every arm. Identical original context (32 XY halo plus median's own z context). Independently check native outputs against direct double-precision patch/offset sums on synthetic data, plus fixed h=30 against earlier sealed development-check decisions.

Only development 2/3/5. Fit on four original calibration planes z=[160,164), x=[360,464), y=[376,632); choose the smallest h maximizing exact Dice per specimen/arm; choose **one** alternative geometry for all three by maximizing mean fit Dice across the three fitted arms (ties in the order listed above). Compare that alternative against the width-based control at its own fitted bandwidth. Do not change the geometry or bandwidth after scoring. Before opening the previously studied check region z=[176,184), x=[512,616), y unchanged for this comparison, publish the raw-derived masks and their hashes, the geometry choice, and all 72 fit confusion matrices. Publish full paired check counts and all changed decisions even if both methods underperform. Promotion requires on **every** scan a Dice gain of at least 0.005 and no precision or recall loss over 0.005, plus baseline equivalence and numerical replay. No formal or reserve input, no change to the formal gate.
