# XY orientation of the existing boundary decoder (no new training)

Retire effective-blur inversion after its monotone screen. Instead test a symmetry of the actual segmentation decoder: the frozen census convolution saw XY flips and transposes during calibration, but it need not give the same decisions when reflected at inference. Do not assume that the archived reference is invariant, or treat agreement as truth. Only the familiar 96-slice transfer ROI of 2/3/5; no formal/reserve evaluation, new labels for training, per-scan choice, retraining or threshold sweep.

Freeze a single alternative: transform the ten **already computed** census input channels and their full XY halo by each of the eight signed permutations of XY (x flip, y flip, then optional transpose, flags 0..7). Do not transform z or rerun the shared preprocessing under the transforms. Invert each orientation on the output lattice; add the unchanged center prior, sum all eight native float32 logits in float64 and threshold the mean at zero (inclusive). No majority-of-hard-labels substitution or choosing a favored orientation. Control is exactly orientation 0, must reproduce all existing census masks. Compute FP64 convolution independently on each oriented feature tensor; publish per-orientation differences including nonfinal disagreements, maximum errors, final count, and votes 0..8 on the unrounded sign. Nonfinite logits, control mismatch or any final CPU/native mismatch preclude promotion. Publish six masks, software/weight identities, realized flips and disagreement maps before opening labels for this experiment.

Separate two questions. (1) Can eight-way averaging lower same-ID volume error P95 by >=2 percentage points on >=5 shared pores on **every** specimen, without either coverage fraction dropping, Dice falling by >.002, or CPU/native final-mask mismatch? Score both arms with the existing eleven metrics, SciPy and native 8/37 slab check. Include lost/gained IDs, all-matched and shared-P95, additions/deletions and their correctness. (2) Are there enough orientation-dependent sites to explain the missed accuracy? Only **after** the seal, use the reference to tally TP/FP/FN/TN by vote count. Count false negatives with 0 pore votes and false positives with 8. Report the best possible voxel Dice treating only sites with 1..7 votes as freely correctable and keeping both unanimous groups fixed. It is an oracle ceiling for voxel Dice, not an attainable detector or a bound on object P95; do not use its mask for measurement or training. If promotion fails, no tuning transform subsets or decision thresholds on these scores. Formal FINAL-GATE and 4/6 remain untouched.
