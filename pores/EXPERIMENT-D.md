# D: axial support at narrow connections — development experiment

Locked recipe before running D on scans 2, 3 and 5. The same XY crop, disjoint labeled calibration slices, evaluation range, physical spacings, eligible-object rule and final thresholds are unchanged. Hypothesis: some large volume errors are due to a one-voxel connection, not a global porosity bias. On C development specimen 3, prediction 3405094 overlaps both reference components 121 (284 of 296 voxels) and 127 (118 of 128); two additional interior reference pores overlap a single boundary-connected prediction. This motivated D, so these are not blind scans.

D uses a native six-output 2-D patch kernel (plain SSD, noise-floor-corrected SSD, absolute differences and a mean-compensated spatial variant). Around each estimated local threshold it measures opposing axial and lateral support, their imbalance and the occupied fraction in three windows. A fixed per-scan calibration recipe gives more weight to low-occupancy neighborhoods. One fixed probability threshold of 0.5; no post-hoc morphology, deletion of small pores or per-evaluation-volume cutoff. Count every split, merge and miss under the original rules; unchanged holdout guard. No claim that the filters reproduce proprietary Avizo internals.
