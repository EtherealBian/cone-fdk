# Two-scale raw residual, prospective development comparison

Can a coarser view of the surrounding material correct isolated pores without creating additional unmatched components? Keep the published census network, denoiser, prior, and weights frozen in all arms. No critical-site loss, label-derived editing, or training on transfer/formal labels. Register before generating new calibration fields or treatment masks. The exact old census control must replay bit-for-bit on the new raw run.

Add one shared pointwise residual head (27→16 ReLU→1, bias, zero final layer, seed 61429). Two equally sized trained arms: `fine` at spacing 1, `coarse` at spacing 4. Use three planes of raw measured intensity (center, mean of z−1 and z+1, absolute difference of z−1 and z+1). Form those three fields *before* averaging. At spacing s average over nonoverlapping s×s blocks with left edges at global scanner multiples of s; bilinearly interpolate between block **centers** to each native output center at the nine offsets in {-2s,0,2s}². Apply the published specimen gray mapping without 8-bit quantization, center and neighbor mean standardized (gray−128)/48, difference gray/48. No ROI-edge padding: read true raw halo. Control has identical channels, ordering, parameter count and training budget, but spacing 1. Also record the unchanged census base as an external fixed comparator. Compute coarse features from the full scanner-coordinate grid, not separately rephased per training patch. On calibration and transfer, require independent scalar direct box sums/interpolation at >=32 distributed centers per scan and scale, including corners; <=1e−5 in normalized features. Test translations by whole blocks and native crop-vs-tile invariance.

Fit from the existing 24 calibration planes per specimen, exactly the sealed census patch/augmentation streams, base BCE + 0.1 tile relative mass loss; head sees native base logits additively with coefficient one. The pointwise head has offset channels in fixed scanner axes: gather head inputs/targets at original locations then apply the same spatial permutation to both (no rotating the offset channel meanings); the frozen base logit is computed on the scanner grid. Both arms use AdamW .002, weight decay .0001, cosine to .0001 over 1536 updates. No per-specimen heads, tuning, early stopping or selection. Publish calibration hashes, weights, six raw-only masks and the replayed base hashes before consulting the reused transfer labels at z=[256,352). Native CUDA vs independent FP64 CPU convolution, including base and both heads, at **every** decision with shared frontend; report maxima and every disagreement count, zero required. Replay metrology at depths 8 and 37 and independently compare each object against SciPy. Score all eleven targets, both coverage denominators, shared IDs and gains/losses. On EACH specimen, coarse vs **each** of fine and fixed census must retain both coverage fractions, lose no more than .002 Dice, and improve same-pore volume P95 by >=2 percentage points on >=5 common pores; no swapping winners. Failure is a research negative. Success only permits formal development; the unchanged FINAL-GATE, independent source replays and public final manifest precede any use of 4/6.
