# Local six-connectivity supervision, development only

The paired tile census helped volume on 2 but lost a pore on 5 when its prediction connected to the crop boundary. Test whether an explicit training signal for local connectivity helps on the complete existing development population, not whether a hand edit rescues one known object. No changes to inference, no reference-dependent postprocessing. No scores on the formal crop or reserved specimens at this stage.

Only the original calibration masks (2/3/5, z=[160,184), x=[360,616), y=[376,632)) may supply supervision. For each center whose entire 3x3x3 neighborhood is inside the calibration crop, temporarily remove the center, keep foreground pore neighbors, and count how many distinct six-connected components of that shell touch a face of the center. At least two means a critical center: foreground (a local connector to preserve) or background (a local gap to preserve). Interior reconnections outside this cube are unknown: this is not a global articulation oracle. Before training require at least 32 distinct centers of EACH sign spanning at least four different z planes for EACH scan. Publish exact signed counts, number of planes and per-plane counts even if sparse; if inadequate, stop this experiment, do not relax the eligibility cutoff.

Use the already sealed census schedule, all patches, augmentations, initialization, axial channels, optimizer, seed 61429, 1536 updates, fixed prior, threshold and both base losses unchanged. The control is the published census weights/masks (re-run the control from weights in raw inference and compare mask hashes). Treatment adds 0.10 times the mean of the per-sign BCE-with-logits means on critical centers actually visited in a batch, averaging only nonempty signs, zero if neither is present. No oversampling of these sites or altered inference architecture. Record supervised exposures for each sign; never report exposures as independent sites. Compare signed training identifications to an independent scalar connectivity implementation.

Freeze the weights and all six raw-only transfer predictions (z=[256,352)) on the public branch before evaluating new outputs against reference labels. Native CUDA and independent CPU FP64 convolution at every decision, shared gray frontend; no exceptions for near-zero flips. Two native slab depths 8/37 + SciPy metrology comparison. Count both-sided coverage, every gained/lost ID, all eleven targets and paired P95. Advance only if EVERY specimen has >=5 common matches, at least 2 percentage points better same-pore volume P95, neither coverage fraction lower, Dice no more than .002 lower, identical native/CPU decisions. Underpowered or failed cases are not replaced. The decision permits a formal development test only; the FINAL-GATE and holdout seal remain binding.
