# Can an XY section estimate tell which whole-pore boundary to keep?

One prospective experiment in the familiar 2/3/5 crop only. No new depth, formal development slabs, or reserved specimens. The chord experiment found complementary endpoints but sparse complete calibration pores. Do not use its score triples, oracle, any migration score, evaluation ID, specimen ID as feature, or known outlier list. Preserve both frozen endpoints (curvature; full oriented face) unchanged. This differs from the old section-area pilot: do not retrain segmentation or let separate slices alter pore topology. Choose a whole existing 3D mask for each existing eligible object.

**Fit.** Recompute the curvature detector from archived original raw calibration [160,184), verify its full mask against the pre-existing calibration digest. Pair each XY plane's 4-connected detector/reference regions with 4..2048 pixels on both sides and at least three pixels of XY margin. Greedy descending IoU (minimum 1/2), tie by increasing detector then reference raster label. Do not reject a 2D component because its 6-connected 3D parent touches a z or xy face. Keep every matched section including exact areas. Count distinct predicted parents and clipped parents separately; sections are not independent pores. Equal total weight per specimen, within it per contributing detector parent, within that per section. If any specimen has no training parent abort, not a two-specimen substitute.

Estimate signed area correction per unit of perimeter, y=(reference area - detector area) / number of exposed 4-connected pixel edges. Use intercept, inverse square root of area, compactness 4πA/P², and the following fixed local gray features on a single raw XY plane. For the section S define B as its inner 4-connected boundary; O as one-pixel outside and H as the second through third outside rings; exclude other predicted foreground from O,H. Core = S minus B, reverting to S if empty. Let L=median(core), U=median(H) in raw gray, K=max(1,U-L); let N(x)=clip((x-L)/K,-2,3). Use mean N(B), mean N(O), their difference, standard deviations N(B),N(O), fraction of B and O below 0.5, and local contrast K in the frozen source gray mapping (K*scale/64 clipped to [0,4]). Empty O or H: unavailable section, zero correction. Fit one shared weighted ridge with intercept unpenalized, all other coefficients penalty 0.02, in these fixed units, no standardization or sweep. Quantize coefficients to 1e-6; predict section correction = perimeter times predicted y clipped to [-1,1]. Round each correction to 1/1024 pixel, nearest with ties to even, before summing. Dump weights, all training features/targets, section counts and signed estimates. Do not infer uncaptured total volume for a calibration parent.

**Predict.** For every eligible, complete curvature object ≥64 voxels sum predicted corrections from all its planar sections; unsupported sections contribute zero. Choose the full face endpoint only if its integer volume is strictly closer than curvature volume to the resulting estimate; ties keep curvature. Use geometry-only bijection. Preserve every untouched component, clipping flag and connectivity; verify selected voxel sets exactly, not only counts. Prediction uses raw scan and sealed parents, no migration reference. Run the complete predictor twice from source and seal decisions, masks, model and software identity publicly before scoring. Native CUDA / SciPy, slabs 8 and 37, twice score from archives, publish full results regardless. Report all eleven gates, matches on both sides, common-ID P95, gains/losses and retrospective endpoint decision accuracy (exclude equal endpoint losses, show denominator). Advancement: all three have calibration support; every specimen passes prior no-loss/coverage/Dice/≥2-point common-P95-improvement screen and no worsening of all-matched P95. No tuning from this test; a research screen is not release acceptance. [Final gate](FINAL-GATE.md) unchanged.
