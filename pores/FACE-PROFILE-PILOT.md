# Locate each oriented pore/material face from its raw intensity line profile

Development only. Keep the frozen curvature detector. Unlike estimates constrained to old masks, displacement uses raw uint16 intensities and can change volumes even without another matched donor. Do not use any donor to set a target. Use exactly the same rule for every specimen and object. Only calibration [160,184) and familiar transfer [256,352), x=[360,616), y=[376,632), specimens 2/3/5. Do not consult the second depth, formal ROI or any reserved specimen. This is digital boundary localization, not physical certification.

**Learn what a centered face looks like.** For every 6-neighbor oriented pore→material interface wholly inside the original calibration crop (no artificial crop faces), last pore voxel p and unit outward d, evaluate three faces p+j*d with j=-1,0,+1. For each face take eight raw samples at signed offsets -3..4 from its anchor, and eight 3x3 tangential-plane sums over the same offsets, each plane perpendicular to d. True five-voxel raw halo in every direction. Normalize each of the two length-eight profiles independently: floor(255*(v-min)/(max-min)), flat → 0. Same integer features for all scans and faces, no spatial coordinates or scan-specific thresholds. Test native CUDA vs independent NumPy at every scored site, plus direct scalar geometry checks and synthetic axis/contrast tests.

For j=-1,+1 take pair difference D=(features(0)-features(j))/255. Fit one shared linear ranker minimizing mean (D*w-1)^2 with equal weight per specimen and equal weight per pair within a specimen, plus 0.01*||w||². Accumulate integer sufficient statistics, solve the 16x16 system in float64, freeze weights rounded to nearest integer after multiplying by 65536. Maximize the integer score for each detector face among -1,0,+1; ties prefer 0 then -1 then +1. No testing other models, thresholds, iterated updates or per-scan calibration. Record calibration pair count and realized preference for true over displaced faces; calibration accuracy is not a transfer result.

**Convert displacements into whole-pore volumes.** Each direction's outward displacement lengthens an axis chord, inward shortens it. Sum the two directions along each axis, then average over three axes (each axis alone integrates the complete volume by summing chord lengths). Target original detector volume plus this mean signed displacement rounded to nearest integer. Report each of the three axis estimates separately; this is a first-order discrete estimator, not a guarantee of consistent shifted geometry. Only interior detector components >=64 may change. For growth, only consider exterior sites proposed by an outward-moving face, not on crop face or within Manhattan distance two of another original component (including small/clipped). For shrinkage, only consider pore sites proposed by an inward-moving face. Order by number of proposing faces descending, then sum of their integer score gains over staying descending, then increasing global z,y,x. Add/remove exactly the required prefix, no partial application: keep original if target <64, not enough sites, or disconnected. Record target, actual, per-axis displacement, vote counts, change digest, and every abstention. Assert whole-volume component bijection, sizes and clipping. Report requested vs realized volume error on the original detector's matched population, including fallbacks. Additionally compare every requested volume against the *previously sealed* donor interval at scoring only; never use that interval in inference.

Publish fit and all masks after two byte-identical source replays, before scoring. Independently score against source labels twice for curvature and line-profile arms via native slabs 8/37 plus SciPy, publish complete per-ID ledger. Screen as in SHELL-VOLUME-PILOT: each specimen no lost matches or reduction in either coverage, shared n>=5 and P95 gain >=2 percentage points, all-match volume P95 no worse, Dice drop <=.002 and exact replay.
