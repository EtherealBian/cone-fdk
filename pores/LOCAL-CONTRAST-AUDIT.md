# Can native local contrast explain which formal H pores were missed?

Development diagnosis only; freeze definitions before measuring signal distributions. H 2/3/5 formal predictions and scores are already public. Do not change H, select objects, train a rule on these observations, move source images or read 4/6. Question: do low-contrast pores dominate the missed references, and can raw contrast separate unmatched predictions from properly measured ones? An unmatched reference of high contrast would advise investigating its topology or size cutoff, not simply sharpening the image.

For **every** >=64-voxel non-face-contact six-connected component in the H predictions and archive reference separately, take the median native uint16 inside its exact support. Build the outside shell of voxels whose minimum Euclidean distance from a component voxel center is in [2,4] inclusive (native voxels). Read real raw halo even when the shell crosses the formal ROI; no zero padding, mask-derived intensity correction, exclusion of other objects, or mixing specimens. Record counts, raw inside/shell medians, shell median absolute deviation, CNR = (median shell − median inside)/(1.4826 × MAD). Undefined for zero MAD; never silently drop. This is robust *local contrast relative to shell variability*, not instrument noise or independent physical detectability. Other pores may occupy part of the shell. Report each row with native seed (first x-fastest support voxel), size, matched status from frozen H, and maximum overlap partner (intersection, size, eligible/face contact). Report all rows, including false positives and misses. Confirm ROI mask hashes, both eligible counts, matches and all native IDs. Independently check every shell by discrete integer squared-offset set union (4..16 inclusion minus the inner squared distances <4), against distance-transform construction on a real raw halo.

In each specimen and each of the two populations separately, compute median CNR for matched and unmatched and the exact pairwise AUC P(matched CNR > unmatched CNR) with half-ties. Require at least 3 independent IDs in EACH group and no undefined CNR for a supported comparison. Prespecify AUC >=.80 on every supported specimen *with all three supported* for interpreting contrast as a consistent ranking; report volume-band counts (<128, 128..511, >=512) and do not claim size-adjusted discrimination. Publish every unmatched reference with CNR >= its specimen's matched reference median as a counterexample to a simple low-contrast interpretation; list its largest overlap rather than assigning cause by eye. Even a good predictive rank cannot create a missing pore or establish final accuracy. Source-reproducible research evidence only.
