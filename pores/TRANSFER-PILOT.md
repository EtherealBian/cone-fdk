# Leave-one-specimen-out boundary transfer (research control)

Registered before reading the new slab labels. E and all 11 final gates remain unchanged. No confirmation data, no new release candidate. We ask whether complete pores from *other* CoCr scans can calibrate the signed volume displacement at an uncertain image boundary. This is extra development annotation, outside the original per-scan 24-slice budget.

- Lock specimens 2, 3, 5; one new slab per specimen, x=[360,616), y=[376,632), z=[256,352). Never join slabs for connectivity. These layers differ from E's fit [160,184) and its previously scored [400,528). Generate and hash all three baseline E float32 probability volumes before reading any new slab labels. Use the original E fit from each scan's original 24 layers, cut 0.5, same CUDA fields and real halos. Do not read specimens 4/6. No replacement slab or extra fit if sparse.
- Calibrate a two-coefficient correction on the other two specimens only. Enumerate whole six-connected predicted components of 32..256 voxels. Let S be the number of inner face-boundary voxels and U = (sum of E probabilities on the outer face shell minus sum of (1-p) on the inner face shell) / S. Outer candidates must see only this label among occupied face neighbors. Fit on unique complete donor reference pairs (reference >=32, prediction 32..256 inclusive, IoU >=0.5, greedy descending IoU then descending prediction/reference labels). Target is (reference voxels - predicted voxels) / S. Minimize squared error averaged equally across contributing specimens and equally across their pairs, plus 0.02 times the sum of squares of both coefficients (intercept and slope). No specimen ID, reference location, or reference size at inference. An empty donor contributes no samples; disclose that and require both donors for promotion. No pairs at all means identity and inconclusive.
- For each predicted target object in that same 32..256 range, round S times the fitted displacement clipped to [-0.25,+0.25] to nearest integer (ties to even). Add highest-p outer voxels or remove lowest-p inner voxels, up to the available candidates. Use ascending source linear index for ties. Apply moves simultaneously; label the final mask again. No repairs for splits, merges, false positives or misses. Never replace hard mask volumes with regression estimates in the scoring tables.
- Retain both remeasured arms, match criterion complete objects >=64 on both sides, IoU >=0.5 one-to-one. For each held-out specimen report eligible counts, missed/unmatched, all-matched volume-error P95, plus the *same* small reference IDs (64..127 voxels inclusive) matched by both masks and their paired P95. List complete/clipped reference support separately, publish source and mask digests, donor fit counts, coefficients and object-level evidence. Keep pairs from different scans distinct. Prediction/scoring are development cross-validation, not three independent confirmations.
- Screen in only if **each** target has at least five shared small references, each of its two donors supplied >=5 pairs, paired small volume-error P95 drops by >=3 percentage points, all-matched P95 does not increase, and neither missed references nor unmatched predictions increases. Insufficient support is inconclusive; do not score the original evaluation region again or open reserved scans unless the research screen passes. Passing would warrant further validation, not a final pass.
