# Locked detector, multi-estimator volume, one face layer — development experiment

Freeze the raw-curvature detector and test an object-volume estimator separately from detection. Familiar development crop ONLY: 2/3/5, x=[360,616), y=[376,632), z=[256,352). No fitting, specimen/ID-specific choices, scoring the second depth, formal ROI, or reserved specimens. Input ONLY the published prediction masks and their manifests. Keep curvature unchanged as the sole comparator. Prespecified donors, once each, in this order: 46-column profile (volume-curvature), census (census-train), strict threshold source (byte-window), axial (axial-raw). These are correlated estimators, not independent observations. No parameter sweep.

1. Label every mask with 6-connectivity. Process only interior detector objects of >=64 voxels. Each donor votes only for a mutually unique eligible whole component with IoU >=1/2 to that detector (test candidates on both sides before assigning; no greedy ID tie). Detector volume plus at least two qualifying donor volumes: target the median voxel count, round an even population's half-voxel upwards. Otherwise keep detector untouched. No hidden minimum percent change.
2. Keep detector geometry except in its original single face-connected boundary layer. For expansion consider exterior voxels sharing a detector face, supported by at least one admitted donor, not on a crop face and not within Manhattan distance two of *any other original detector component*, even below eligibility. For contraction consider original voxels with <6 original same-object face neighbors and disputed by at least one admitted donor. Order expansions by descending admitted donor support, then descending original same-object face neighbors; contractions by ascending both. Break ties by increasing global z,y,x. Take exactly the required prefix, or abstain entirely if insufficient or the resulting object is not 6-connected. This is not iterative erosion/growth. Validate that distinct original detector objects correspond one-to-one after all edits, touch status unchanged, and edited sizes exactly equal the registered targets. Log all eligible objects, qualifying donor IDs/sizes, target, attempted change, eligible edits, reason, actual size and a digest of edited coordinates. Verify support/face counts by independent CPU and CUDA operators; replay predictor from the sealed input masks in two directories.
3. Publish all three edited masks, ledgers, hashes and code before opening this experiment's labels. Independently rerun both arms' raw source-label score with native slab depths 8/37 and independent SciPy checks. Record every gain/loss, both coverage denominators, all 11 gates and shared-reference P95. Screen each specimen vs curvature: no lost matching IDs, no loss on either coverage measure, >=5 paired pores and >=2 percentage-point shared volume P95 improvement, all-match P95 no worse, voxel Dice drop <=0.002, exact replay. All three must pass to advance this mechanism; even then the formal gate is unchanged.
4. Diagnostic only: for the detector's original matches compare actual detector and requested-target volume to the reference, keeping the *same* matched population, including every fallback. Also compute the smallest possible absolute error if a label-aware chooser could pick ANY integer in the closed range spanned by the admitted complete estimates (only detector when fewer than two donors). This is an optimistic bound on the volume estimates, not a prediction or a geometry/coverage guarantee. Report reference IDs outside that interval by more than 15%; do not use them to select methods per specimen/ID. Publish negative results.
